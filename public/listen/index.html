Listen to the eight song motifs

We analysed 116,792 songs from 3,160 passerine species. Their acoustic structure forms a continuum that concentrates around eight poles, the acoustic motifs. Birds combine these building blocks into their own songs. Tap ▶ to hear each one.

Each recording is shown as a spectrogram: time runs from left to right (12 s) and high pitches are at the top. The frequency range is adapted to each motif and given below its spectrogram. Tap a spectrogram to play from that point.
